This study collected 916 earthquake events with magnitudes of 3.0 and above recorded by 5,134 low-cost MEMS sensors between January 2024 and December 2025,
during the operational period of the China Earthquake Intensity Rapid Reporting and Earthquake Early Warning Engineering,
including 14,075 sets of three-component acceleration waveforms, with a maximum focal depth of 148 km and a hypocentral distance of 401.8 km
